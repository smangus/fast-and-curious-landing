The Opportunity

Meet the Workforce Training Fund Program

The Massachusetts Workforce Training Fund Program (WTFP) Express Grant exists so companies like yours can invest in employee skills without eating the cost. It's funded by employer contributions the state already collects — your company may already be paying into it.

100%
of tuition covered for employers with 1–50 MA employees
50%

How It Works

Three steps to a fully-funded seat

The application is submitted by your employer, not by you — which means your job is just to get the ball rolling.

1

Check the basics

Your company is likely eligible if it's based in Massachusetts, has 100 or fewer MA employees, and pays into the state unemployment insurance system — which is standard for nearly every MA employer.

2

Bring it to your manager or HR/L&D

Pitch it as what it is: a way to build in-demand AI skills on your team's roadmap, at little to no cost to the company. Use the talking points below to make the ask easy.

3

Your employer applies — ahead of the cohort start

Your company (not you) submits the Express Grant application to Commonwealth Corporation, the state agency that administers the fund. Because training can only begin after the grant is awarded, the sooner this starts, the better — so raise it now rather than the week before the cohort kicks off.

Make The Ask

You're the best advocate for your own development

Nobody knows better than you that this training moves the needle on your work. Here's a short message you can adapt and send to your manager or People team today.

Sample message to your manager

Hi [Manager] — I found an 8-week hands-on AI upskilling program built specifically for bench scientists (The Fast and the Curious, $2,500, biotech-specific curriculum covering protein structure prediction, molecular design, and more). I'd love to enroll, and I think we can get it fully or mostly covered by the state: Massachusetts' Workforce Training Fund Program (Express Grant) reimburses eligible MA employers 100% of training costs like this for companies our size. The company applies (not me), and Commonwealth Corporation typically decides within about 3 weeks. Could we look into applying? I'm happy to pull together whatever details are needed — I just want to get the request in early since training can't start until the grant is approved. Eligibility, reimbursement percentages, and award decisions are determined solely by Commonwealth Corporation, the Massachusetts agency that administers the Workforce Training Fund Program. Grants must be applied for and awarded before training begins; funds are capped at $15,000 per company per year and $3,000 per employee per course. The Fast and the Curious is not affiliated with Commonwealth Corporation or the Commonwealth of Massachusetts. Visit commcorp.org for current, authoritative program rules.
